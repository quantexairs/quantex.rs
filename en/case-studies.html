Featured case study
Food industry Custom lead generation app

From a local brand to shelves in nearly every store across Serbia

The problem

A Serbian healthy food producer had excellent products but minimal distribution reach. Sales depended entirely on personal connections and a slow manual process: someone on the team would individually call or email stores each day, with no pipeline, no tracking, no system. They were contacting 40 to 60 outlets per month, converting fewer than 10% of them. Growth was blocked by team capacity, not product quality.

The solution

We built a custom web application for automated lead generation and management in retail:

  • Automatic mapping and outreach to retail outlets by region and category across Serbia
  • Personalized pitches tailored to store type, with automated follow-up sequences
  • CRM pipeline tracking every contact from first email to signed distribution agreement
  • Sales team only receives warm leads who have already shown interest — no more cold calling

"In six months our products appeared on shelves from the north to the south of Serbia. Doing this manually would have taken us years."

Head of Sales, Serbian food producer
Measured results
~100% of retail regions in Serbia reached within 6 months
8x more outlets contacted per month without growing the team
3.2x improvement in conversion rate thanks to warm leads
6 mo. from first implementation to full nationwide distribution
Investment paid back in 3.5 months
