Common questions

Answers to the questions
clients ask us most often

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is a set of technical and content changes on a site that help Google understand what the site is about and rank it higher for relevant searches. For a local business, that means being found by customers who are already searching for your services in your city, without paying for every click.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is optimizing content so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews recognize, understand, and cite it as a source for their answers. While SEO targets the classic list of search results, AEO targets the direct answer a user gets from an AI system, without clicking through to the site. We achieve this with structured data, a clear FAQ structure, and content written as a direct answer to questions.

The first changes in indexing and technical site health are visible within the first few weeks. Movement in search rankings usually starts after 6 to 12 weeks, and steady growth continues as content and site authority build up. SEO is a process, not a one-time change.

It depends on the state of your existing site. If it's technically healthy but poorly positioned, SEO and AEO optimization is often enough. If the site is outdated, slow, or converts visitors into inquiries poorly, a redesign and rebuild deliver a better long-term result. The first conversation is exactly for this: to assess what's more cost-effective for your case.

A website primarily presents information and brand, it showcases services, builds trust, and generates inquiries. A web application has interactive functionality: user accounts, data processing, internal tools, or systems used by employees or clients. Many projects start as a website and gain application-like functionality over time.

A smaller business site typically takes 2 to 4 weeks from an agreed design to launch. Web applications depend on the scope of functionality, simpler projects take 4 to 8 weeks, more complex ones longer. We work iteratively, with regular progress reviews instead of you waiting for a final product.

Yes. After launch we offer monthly maintenance covering hosting, security updates, minor content changes, and ongoing tracking of SEO and AEO performance. The goal is for the site to stay fast, secure, and visible, not to be forgotten after launch.

Price depends on scope: number of pages, design complexity, whether custom functionality is needed, and whether SEO/AEO optimization is included alongside the build. After the first conversation you get a concrete quote with a clear price, no hidden costs.

No agency can honestly guarantee an exact position, because no third party controls the search algorithm. What we can guarantee is a technically healthy site, relevant content, and a consistent SEO/AEO strategy, the approach we applied for our client angelicphoto.rs, where we achieved the #1 position for all key services in Pančevo.
