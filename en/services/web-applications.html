What is a custom web application

A custom web application isn't off-the-shelf software adjusted until it roughly fits your process. It's a system designed from scratch around the specific steps of your business, the fields, roles, rules, and workflows you define.

Instead of changing the way you work to fit a tool, the tool is built around the way you already work. That means fewer workarounds, less manual data entry between systems, and a clearer picture of what's actually happening in the company.

This is especially valuable when your process has specifics that no off-the-shelf solution fully covers.

Custom functionality, not an off-the-shelf fit

Every screen and workflow is designed around your specific process, not a generic template.

User accounts and access control

Different roles (admin, employee, client) see and do exactly what they're permitted to.

Integration with your existing tools

The application connects to the accounting, CRM, or other systems you already use, instead of duplicating them.

Architecture that grows with your business

The application is built so new features and a growing number of users can be added without rebuilding the system.

The Difference

Off-the-shelf software vs a custom-built application

What off-the-shelf software is

Off-the-shelf SaaS solutions offer a broad set of features built to fit a large number of different companies at once. That means none of those companies gets exactly what it needs, you pay a monthly subscription for features you'll never use, while the part of your process that's specific to you gets patched together with spreadsheets and manual steps.

As the company grows, those limitations become more visible, and switching providers comes with data migration and a new learning curve.

  • You pay a subscription for features you don't use
  • Part of the process is still handled manually or in spreadsheets
  • You're limited by someone else's product roadmap

What a custom-built application is

An application built by Quantex is designed around your specific process from the very first screen. There are no unnecessary features that get in the way, and nothing missing that you have to compensate for manually.

You define the development priorities, and the architecture is built to grow alongside your company, without dependence on someone else's roadmap or pricing policy.

  • You pay only for the functionality you actually need
  • The process is automated exactly the way your team works
  • You decide what gets built and when

What the development process looks like

Development starts with a conversation where we map your existing process and requirements, who the application's users are, what steps they perform, and where the biggest bottlenecks are in the current way of working.

Based on that, we create a design and feature plan that you review and approve before development even begins, so there are no surprises later in the project.

Development itself happens in phases with regular testing, and the project wraps up with launch and training for your team on day-to-day use of the application.

  • Mapping the process and requirements through conversations with your team
  • Design and feature plan for review before development starts
  • Phased development with continuous testing
  • Launch and team training on using the application
