What AEO is and why it matters

The way people look for information is changing. Instead of typing a short query and scanning a list of links, people increasingly ask an AI assistant a full question and expect a direct, ready-made answer - without ever needing to visit a website.

For a business, that means a new kind of visibility: not ranking on the first page of search results, but being the source an AI assistant references or cites within the answer itself. For that to happen, content has to be structured and written in a way AI systems can easily understand, extract, and reliably pass along.

AEO doesn't replace SEO - it extends it to a new channel for discovering information.

Structured data and schema

Markup that clearly tells AI systems what a page is about and how the information is organized.

Content written as a direct answer

Sentences and paragraphs formulated to answer a specific question on their own, ready to be cited.

The Difference

SEO vs AEO

What SEO targets

Classic SEO targets your website's position in the list of search results. The goal is for the user to see your link, click it, and land on your site to explore your offering further.

This still accounts for a large share of traffic and remains the foundation of digital visibility - a technically sound, fast, and well-structured site is a prerequisite for both approaches.

  • Focus on ranking position in the results list
  • Goal is a click and a visit to the site
  • Measured by rank, clicks, and traffic

What AEO targets

AEO targets having your content become the actual answer an AI assistant gives a user, or being cited as the source of that answer. The user often gets the information directly, without a click, but your brand becomes part of the answer they see.

SEO and AEO aren't competitors - they're two complementary channels through which users get informed today, and a good foundation for one almost always helps the other.

  • Focus on having content cited in an AI answer
  • Goal is brand recognition and mention
  • Measured by appearance and citation in AI answers

Who benefits most from AEO

Services with frequent questions

Lawyers, consultants, healthcare

Clients of these services often ask an AI assistant a question before contacting anyone - about the process, their rights, pricing, or options. Clearly structured content increases the chance that your answer is the one the AI assistant passes along.

Higher chance of being mentioned before first contact
Local business

Shops, services, and local companies

When a user asks an AI assistant for a recommendation in their city or area, well-structured information about hours, services, and location increases the chance that the assistant mentions you at all.

Greater visibility in locally oriented queries
B2B and SaaS companies

Companies with a complex offering

B2B buyers increasingly use AI assistants to compare solutions before contacting sales. Clearly articulated content about features and differences from competitors increases the chances you'll be part of that comparison.

Presence in AI-assisted buyer research

How we carry out AEO

The process starts with a detailed audit of your site's existing content - we check which topics you already cover, how they're phrased, and where clear answers are missing to the questions your prospective clients are actually asking.

We then structure and expand the content: adding schema markup, building clear FAQ sections, and rewriting key paragraphs so they answer a specific question on their own, ready to be picked up by AI systems.

After that, we track whether and how your content appears in AI assistant answers, and continuously adjust the approach - this is a fast-changing field, so we treat AEO as an ongoing process, not a one-time project.

  • Audit of current content and identification of gaps
  • Structuring content and implementing schema markup
  • Tracking appearance and citation in AI answers
  • Continuous adjustment as AI models and platforms evolve
