How It Works

From identification to qualified lead

Step 1

Find and identify

The system searches relevant databases, LinkedIn, industry directories, and intent signals to find companies and people matching your ideal customer profile. Criteria are built together based on your best clients.

Step 2

Personalized outreach

Every message is personalized based on publicly available information about the recipient and their company. Timing is optimized. Tone is matched to the platform and the context.

Step 3

Follow-up and qualification

The system tracks all responses, qualifies intent, and handles the initial part of the conversation. When a lead meets your criteria, it hands them off to your team with full context.
