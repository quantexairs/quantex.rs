The Difference

Ready-made template vs a custom-built site

What a ready-made template is

Ready-made templates are quick to launch, but they come with trade-offs. Structure and design are predefined, so adapting them to your brand is limited to swapping colors and a logo. Your competitors are likely using the same template.

The technical SEO foundation in most templates is generic and not built around your specific content and target search terms, leaving room for slower loading and weaker search visibility.

  • Limited customization of design and structure
  • Generic look, similar to competitors
  • Weak technical SEO foundation without extra intervention

What a Quantex custom-built site is

A custom-built site is built around your brand, content, and business goal from the very first line of code. Page structure, loading speed, and SEO foundations are planned together with the design, not separately from it.

The result is a site that looks different from your competitors, runs faster, and grows more easily alongside your business - new pages, new features, new content without technical roadblocks.

  • Design and structure tailored to your brand
  • Built for speed and search engines from the start
  • Scalable - grows together with your business
