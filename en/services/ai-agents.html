What's the difference between an AI agent and ChatGPT

ChatGPT knows nothing about your business. It draws on general knowledge and has no access to your pricing, internal procedures, or the history of the client who just reached out to you.

An agent built by Quantex is trained exclusively on your data. It knows your services, pricing, terms, and sales process. It knows what to say when a client asks anything that's relevant to your business.

That's not a matter of degree. It's a different category entirely.

How the agent is trained on your data

The process starts with collecting all relevant documents from your organization. That includes policies, pricing, service descriptions, FAQ pages, client conversation history, and any other sources relevant to the agent's role.

This material goes through a processing and vectorization pipeline, then is used to train or fine-tune a base language model. The result is an agent with deep, specific knowledge of your organization's context.

Before going live, we run multi-stage testing against real-world scenarios to verify accuracy, consistency, and the limits of the agent's knowledge.
